ENGINE ARCHITECTURE

Engineered to Handle What Other Cleaners Can't

Traditional disk utilities freeze when processing 100,000+ files. SpaceClean scans smoothly in safe parts so your PC stays fast and responsive.

🧠

Smart Memory Guard

Scans massive drives in smooth parts (~20k files or ~25 GB). If a folder is huge, SpaceClean pauses gently so you can clean what was scanned first without choking your RAM.

Chronological Time Waves

Breaks multi-gigabyte folders into years (2022, 2023, 2024, 2025). Sort by Oldest Years First, Largest + Oldest, or Clutter Density to reclaim storage safely.
